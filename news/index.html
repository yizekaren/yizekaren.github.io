News

Updates from Yize Zhao and the Zhao research group.

Yize Zhao · Yale

2026

  1. September 2026

    Two papers from our group have been accepted at the 2026 Conference on Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS): “Orthogonal Sparse Subgraph Alignment for Structure-Function Coupling in Brain Networks” and “ELPAC: Endpoint-Anchored Latent Progression with Stage-Varying Multimodal Coordination”.

  2. September 2026

    Our paper, “A Bayesian deep learning framework for brain dynamic functional connectivity linked with cognition,” by Xinyuan Tian, Yuanshuang Guo, Shengxian Ding, Yifei Zhang, Denise Esserman, and Yize Zhao, is accepted for publication in Biometrics.

  3. September 2026

    Congratulations to our group's former postdoc Selena Wang on receiving an NIH R01 award as principal investigator at Indiana University! Yize Zhao serves as a co-investigator on the project.

  4. Fall 2026

    Congratulations to our group's former postdoc Zhiling Gu on starting a tenure-track assistant professorship in Statistics at the University of South Carolina!

  5. Fall 2026

    Congratulations to our group's former postdoc Simiao Gao on starting a tenure-track assistant professorship at Augusta University!

  6. August 2026

    Welcome Yang Long, Jae Chang, and Fangyi Wang as postdoctoral researchers in the group!

  7. August 2026

    Yize Zhao gives an invited presentation at the Joint Statistical Meetings (JSM) in Boston.

  8. July 2026

    Yize Zhao gives an invited presentation at the International Biometric Conference (IBC) in Seoul, South Korea.

  9. July 2026

    BHPI, led by Shengxian (Naomi) Ding with Haonan Gao, Pangpang Liu, Xinyuan Tian, and Yize Zhao, is featured as an oral paper at the International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML).

  10. June 2026

    Welcome Luyang Fang as a postdoctoral researcher in the group!

  11. June 2026

    Shengxian (Naomi) Ding, Emily Johns, Anton Orlichenko, and collaborators publish a Bayesian framework linking brain connectivity and shape in The Annals of Applied Statistics.

  12. June 2026

    Yize Zhao, Xinyuan Tian, and Zhihao Wu give invited presentations at the Statistical Methods in Imaging (SMI) conference at the University of Michigan in Ann Arbor.

  13. June 2026

    Congratulations to Yumeng Yan on admission to the PhD program in Biomathematics at UCLA!

  14. June 2026

    Congratulations to Eric Gao on starting a new position as a Data Scientist at Polymer Capital Management!

  15. June 2026

    Yize Zhao is elected 2027 Program Chair-Elect of the American Statistical Association's Statistics in Imaging Section.

  16. May 2026

    Yize Zhao is named a 2026 Scholar in Diagnostic Excellence by the National Academy of Medicine.

  17. April 2026

    A study jointly led by Simiao Gao, Zhiling Gu, and Shengxian (Naomi) Ding connects brain organization with development, behavior, and genetics in Nature Communications.

  18. January 2026

    Congratulations to alumna Yiting Wang, winner, and PhD student Xinyuan Tian, runner-up, in the American Statistical Association's Statistics in Imaging Student Paper Competition!
